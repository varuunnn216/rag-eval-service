# Meridian City Transit Authority Rider Handbook, 2026 Edition

## About MCTA

The Meridian City Transit Authority (MCTA) was founded in 1974 and has its headquarters at 400 Civic Plaza. MCTA runs four Metro lines, 38 bus routes, two Harbor Ferry routes and a paratransit service called MCTA Access. The 38 bus routes are made up of 29 local routes (numbered 1 to 29), 6 express routes (E1 to E6) and 3 Night Owl routes (N1 to N3).

The service area is divided into three fare zones. Zone 1 is Downtown. Zone 2 covers the inner suburbs. Zone 3 covers the outer suburbs and the airport.

## Fares

### Single Fares

| Mode  | Zone 1 only | Zones 1-2 | Zones 1-3 |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| Metro | 2.50 USD    | 3.75 USD  | 5.25 USD  |
| Bus   | 2.00 USD    | 2.75 USD  | 3.50 USD  |
| Ferry | 4.50 USD flat for any trip | | |

Express bus routes charge a surcharge of 1.00 USD on top of the normal bus fare. Night Owl routes charge a flat fare of 3.00 USD regardless of zone. Trips that start or end at Airport Station (Blue Line) have an airport surcharge of 2.00 USD added to the fare.

### Passes

| Pass         | Price         | How long it is valid                    |
|--------------|---------------|------------------------------------------|
| Day Pass     | 9.00 USD      | 24 hours from the first tap              |
| Weekly Pass  | 32.00 USD     | 7 days from the first tap                |
| Monthly Pass | 110.00 USD    | One calendar month                       |
| Annual Pass  | 1,100.00 USD  | 12 months                                |

All passes are valid in all three zones and on Metro, Bus and Ferry. Pass holders ride Night Owl routes at no extra cost. The airport surcharge is not waived for Day Pass holders, but it is waived for Weekly, Monthly and Annual pass holders.

### Fare Capping

With tap-and-go fare capping, a rider never pays more than the price of a Day Pass (9.00 USD) in one day, or more than the price of a Weekly Pass (32.00 USD) in any rolling 7-day period. Fare capping only works when you tap the same registered Meridian Card or the same phone each time.

### Reduced Fares and Free Travel

- Seniors aged 65 or older and riders with disabilities pay 50 percent of the single fare and get 30 percent off all passes. They need a Reduced Fare Card, which is free. The card is requested at the Central Terminal customer office and takes 10 business days to process.
- Students in grades K to 12 ride the bus for free every day with a valid student ID.
- College students get 25 percent off the Monthly Pass with a valid college ID.
- Children under 6 ride free. Each paying adult can bring up to 2 children under 6.

### Transfers

A transfer between Bus and Metro is free if it is made within 90 minutes of the first tap. Transfers that involve the Ferry are valid for 120 minutes. A transfer cannot be used to return on the same route in the opposite direction.

## Paying for Your Trip

### The Meridian Card

The Meridian Card costs 5 USD and the price is not refundable. The first load must be at least 5 USD, and the maximum balance is 200 USD. Riders can also use the MCTA Go mobile app, which works like the card.

Cash is accepted on buses only and exact change is required, because drivers cannot give change. Ferry ticket booths also accept cash. Metro stations do not accept cash at the gates.

### Lost or Stolen Cards

If a card is registered online, the balance is protected. A replacement card costs 5 USD, and the old balance is moved to the new card within 3 business days. The balance of an unregistered card cannot be recovered.

Central Terminal is the only station served by three lines: Red, Blue and Green. The Red and Gold lines meet at Museum Square. The Green and Gold lines meet at Eastgate. Harbor Point is the end of the Red Line and also the main Ferry terminal.

### Riding the Bus

Board at the front door and tap your card at the reader. Pull the cord to request a stop. Buses can carry up to 2 bikes on the front rack at any time of day. All buses have a ramp for wheelchairs and strollers.

## Harbor Ferry

The Harbor Ferry has two routes, both starting at Harbor Point. Route F1 goes to Seaview Island and takes 25 minutes. Route F2 goes to Marina Bay and takes 40 minutes. The F1 vessel carries up to 150 passengers and the F2 vessel carries up to 220 passengers.

Ferries run from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM, every 30 minutes. From November 1 to March 31 the winter schedule applies: ferries run every 60 minutes and the last sailing is at 7:00 PM. Sailings are cancelled when winds are above 40 knots. Bikes travel on the ferry free of charge at any time.

## Accessibility

All Metro stations have elevators and are step-free, except Old Quarter on the Green Line, which is being renovated until June 2027. All buses have ramps, priority seating and audio and visual stop announcements. Service animals are always allowed on every mode.

### MCTA Access Paratransit

MCTA Access is a door-to-door service for riders who cannot use regular routes because of a disability. The fare is a flat 2.50 USD per trip. Trips must be booked by 5:00 PM on the day before travel. The eligibility application takes 21 days to process. The service operates within three quarters of a mile of a fixed route.

## Bikes, Scooters and Luggage

Bikes are allowed on Metro trains outside peak hours, which means they are not allowed on weekdays from 6:30 AM to 9:30 AM and from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. Bikes are allowed all day on weekends. Folding bikes are allowed on every mode at any time if they are folded. Electric scooters are allowed only when folded and carried.

Luggage larger than 80 x 50 x 30 cm is not allowed on Metro trains during peak hours. The Blue Line to the airport is exempt from this rule, and luggage of any size is allowed there. Only small pets in closed carriers are allowed. Service animals do not need a carrier.

## Lost and Found

Lost items are kept for 30 days. The Lost Property Office is at Central Terminal on Level B1. It is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. It is closed on Sunday.

Items found on a bus first go to the bus depot, and it takes 2 business days for them to reach Central Terminal. Items found on Metro or at stations go to Central Terminal on the same day. To claim an item, fill in the online claim form. Each claim gets a reference number in the format LF-YYYY-NNNNN.

The retrieval fee is 5 USD per item. Wallets, ID documents and phones are returned free of charge. Items valued above 500 USD are kept for 90 days instead of 30. After the holding period, unclaimed items are donated to charity.

## Holidays and Special Events

On New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, MCTA runs the Sunday schedule, and the Gold Line does not run. On New Year's Eve, Metro runs all night and rides are free from 10:00 PM to 4:00 AM.

## Policy Changes in 2026

- Effective January 1, 2026, the Metro fare for Zone 1 rose from 2.25 USD to 2.50 USD. The Monthly Pass rose from 105 USD to 110 USD and the Annual Pass rose from 1,050 USD to 1,100 USD. Bus fares did not change.
- Effective March 1, 2026, the Night Owl flat fare rose from 2.50 USD to 3.00 USD.
- Effective July 1, 2026, the free transfer window between Bus and Metro was extended from 75 minutes to 90 minutes.

## Developer API

MCTA offers a free real-time data API for developers. API keys are requested on the developer portal and are approved within 2 business days. The base URL is https://api.mcta.example/v2 and the main endpoints are /arrivals, /alerts and /routes.

```http
GET https://api.mcta.example/v2/arrivals?stop_id=STN-0142&limit=5
X-API-Key: <your key>
```

Each API key is limited to 60 requests per minute. Arrival data is refreshed every 15 seconds. The static GTFS feed is updated every Monday at 2:00 AM.

| Status code | Meaning                         |
|-------------|---------------------------------|
| 401         | The API key is invalid          |
| 404         | The stop_id is unknown          |
| 429         | The rate limit was exceeded     |

## Glossary

- **Tap-on:** touching a card or phone on the reader at the start of a trip.
- **Fare capping:** the rule that limits what a rider pays in a day or a week.
- **Peak:** weekday hours from 6:30 AM to 9:30 AM and from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM.
- **Paratransit:** door-to-door transport for riders who cannot use regular routes.
- **GTFS:** a standard data format for public transit schedules.
