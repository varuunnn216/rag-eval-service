# Bluebird Cafe Staff Handbook

## Opening Hours

Bluebird Cafe is open Monday to Friday from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM. On Saturday and Sunday it opens at 9:00 AM and closes at 4:00 PM. The cafe is closed on Diwali and on New Year's Day.

## Staff Roles

### Baristas

Baristas make drinks and keep the espresso machine clean. A new barista shadows a senior barista for 5 days before working a shift alone.

### Cashiers

Cashiers take orders and handle payments. The cashier also checks that every order is read back to the customer before it is sent to the barista.

### Shift Manager

The shift manager opens and closes the cafe, counts the cash drawer and approves refunds above 500 rupees.

## Refund Policy

A customer can get a full refund within 30 minutes of buying a drink if it was made wrong. Cashiers can approve refunds up to 500 rupees. Any refund above 500 rupees must be approved by the shift manager. Bakery items cannot be refunded, but they can be replaced if they are stale.

## Loyalty Program

Customers earn 1 point for every 50 rupees spent. After collecting 10 points, the customer gets one free drink of any size. Points expire after 6 months without a purchase.

## Cleaning Schedule

The espresso machine is cleaned with a backflush every evening at closing time. The milk steam wand is wiped after every single use. The floor is mopped twice a day, once at 12:00 PM and once at closing.

## Allergy Safety

Oat milk and almond milk use separate steaming pitchers. Staff must ask every customer about allergies when they order a drink with nuts or dairy. If a customer reports an allergy, the barista washes their hands and uses a clean pitcher.
