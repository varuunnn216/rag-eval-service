# Harborview University Library Guide

## Locations

Harborview University has two libraries. The Main Library is in Building A. The Science Library is in Building C. Both use the same university ID card for entry and borrowing.

## Opening Hours

Main Library: The Main Library is open Monday to Thursday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and on Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. On Saturday it is open from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. It is closed on Sunday.

Science Library: The Science Library is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. It is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

Exam Weeks: During exam weeks, which are the last two weeks of each semester, the Main Library is open 24 hours from Monday to Friday. The Science Library stays open until midnight on weekdays during exam weeks.

## Borrowing Limits

| Borrower      | Books allowed | Loan period | Renewals  |
|---------------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| Undergraduate | 15            | 21 days     | 2         |
| Graduate      | 30            | 60 days     | 3         |
| Faculty       | 50            | 120 days    | Unlimited |

Before Fall 2026, the loan period for undergraduates was 14 days. It was extended to 21 days starting in Fall 2026. Reference books cannot be borrowed. Course reserve items can only be used inside the library and are loaned for 2 hours at a time.

## Late Fees

A late book costs 0.50 USD per day, up to a maximum of 20 USD per book. Late course reserve items cost 5 USD per hour. A book that is more than 30 days overdue is marked as lost. The borrower then pays the replacement cost plus a 15 USD processing fee.

## Interlibrary Loan

Interlibrary Loan (ILL) lets students get items that Harborview does not own. Follow these steps:

1. Search the catalog. If the item is not available, click Request via ILL.
2. Sign in with your university ID.
3. Fill in the request form and choose a pickup location.
4. Wait for the item. Articles arrive in 2 to 3 business days. Books arrive in 7 to 10 business days.
5. Collect the item within 5 days of the arrival email, or the request is cancelled.

Each borrower can have at most 10 active ILL requests. Textbooks are not available through ILL.

## Study Rooms

Group study rooms can be booked up to 7 days in advance, for a maximum of 2 hours per day. Main Library rooms need at least 3 people. Science Library rooms need at least 2 people. A booking must be cancelled at least 30 minutes before it starts. Three no-shows in one month suspend booking rights for 14 days.

## Printing and Scanning

Black and white printing costs 0.08 USD per page. Color printing costs 0.40 USD per page. Undergraduates get their first 50 black and white pages free each semester. The free allowance does not apply to color pages. Scanning is free for everyone.

## Help Desk

The help desk phone number is 555-0188. It is staffed Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Questions can also be sent to help@harborview-library.example, and replies usually arrive within one business day.
